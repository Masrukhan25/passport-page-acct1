An illustrated passport spread for the Republic of Caelmar, a country that
isn't there. Its capital sits on top of a sea cliff, and the only way in is
the cliff lift — so the passport's defining rule is the cliff-lift transit
endorsement: entry by lift only, 06:00–18:00, closed when the wind exceeds
60 km/h. The holder is Maren Solvig, a licensed cliff-lift operator
(licence L-114), and her stamps tell two round trips: to Rovena in June
2023 and to Telmara in September 2024. Every stamp is part of a matched
entry/exit pair, in chronological order, all after the 14 March 2023 issue
date and inside the passport's ten-year validity.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
